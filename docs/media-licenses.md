# Media licensing

Verified 2026-09-27.

## OOPT Leningrad Region

The official OOPT website publishes a photo gallery and displays © 2026, but no explicit public content-reuse license was found. Its published privacy policy concerns personal data rather than a license to reproduce photographs. Therefore OOPT photographs must remain source-linked only until written reuse permission is obtained.

Contact published by the rights holder: hello@ooptlo.ru.
